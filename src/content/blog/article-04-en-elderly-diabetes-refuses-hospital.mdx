## 2. The Three-Dimensional Entropy Reduction Framework

### 2.1 Dark Matter Layer: Deconstructing the "Ostrich Mentality"
Behind the refusal lies a complex web of behavioral psychology:
- **Survival Fear (Fear of Cost):** For the elderly who lived through scarcity, health expenses feel like a threat to family survival.
- **Cognitive Avoidance (Fear of Diagnosis):** If they don't know the result, they believe the "lion" doesn't exist.
- **Dignity Preservation (Loss of Autonomy):** Seeking help is often interpreted as an admission of being "useless."

**Actionable Shift:** Do not talk about "treatment." Talk about "optimizing daily comfort." Instead of "We need to treat your diabetes," try "Let's use a non-invasive device to monitor why your feet feel tired at night."

- **Evidence:** Studies show that early intervention in diabetic foot care (offloading + glycemic control) reduces amputation risk by over 50% [1, 2].
- **Reframing:** Present treatment as a way to *maintain* their current activity level, not *stop* them from doing things.
